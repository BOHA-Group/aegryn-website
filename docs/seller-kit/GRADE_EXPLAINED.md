# GRADE EXPLAINED — How to Read Your Aegryn Certification Report

> **Audience:** Sellers — transmitted with the grade report  
> **Version:** CIFS v1.0

---

## Your Grade at a Glance

Your Aegryn grade is the result of the **CIFS certification** — a structured audit across 4 dimensions:
**Code · IP & Rights · Finance · Security**

Each dimension is evaluated independently. The final grade reflects the overall maturity and transferability of your asset.

---

## The Grade Scale

| Grade | Meaning for buyers |
|---|---|
| **AEG ★** | Apex. The highest standard — institutional-grade asset, exceptional on all dimensions. Rare. |
| **AAA** | Gold. Premium asset with strong fundamentals across all 4 dimensions. High buyer confidence. |
| **AA** | Silver. Solid, well-documented asset with minor improvement areas. Strong market positioning. |
| **A** | Blue. Standard asset — viable and transferable, with some areas to strengthen. |
| **B** | Amber. Early-stage or maturing asset. Transferable but requires buyer awareness of open items. |
| **Non-certifiable** | The asset cannot be certified in its current state. See refusal reasons for remediation path. |

---

## What Each Dimension Covers

### C — Code & Architecture
Assesses the technical solidity of your asset: codebase quality, test coverage, architecture scalability, CI/CD automation, documentation, and vulnerability management.

A strong C score signals to buyers that the asset is maintainable, scalable, and safe to operate post-transfer.

### I — IP & Rights
Verifies that all intellectual property is cleanly owned and transferable: trademarks, employee IP assignments, open-source licence compliance, third-party API contracts, and GDPR/LPD status.

A strong I score means there are no hidden legal risks in the transfer.

### F — Finance
Validates the commercial traction: ARR level and history, churn, NRR, gross margin, growth, client diversification, and financial runway.

A strong F score gives buyers confidence in the revenue quality and predictability.

### S — Security
Evaluates the security posture: pentest recency, MFA on admin access, encryption, GDPR documentation, and absence of active incidents.

A strong S score assures buyers that the asset meets baseline security standards expected for a professional digital operation.

---

## How the Grade Is Calculated

The four dimensions are equally weighted. Your total score determines your grade:

| Score range | Grade |
|---|---|
| 90–100 | AEG ★ |
| 75–89 | AAA |
| 60–74 | AA |
| 45–59 | A |
| 30–44 | B |
| Below 30 | Non-certifiable |

> **Note:** Certain critical conditions trigger an **automatic refusal** regardless of score — for example, an active security incident or unresolved IP litigation combined with missing assignment contracts. These are listed explicitly in your report if applicable.

---

## The Public Rationale

Your grade report includes a **public rationale** — a concise qualitative summary that Aegryn may share with qualified buyers. It describes your asset's strengths without disclosing sensitive metrics or internal scoring details.

Example: *"This asset presents a solid technical architecture with robust test coverage. The intellectual property is correctly protected with no active litigation. Financial metrics demonstrate established commercial traction. The security posture meets the standards expected for a professional digital operation."*

---

## What Happens If Your Asset Is Not Certified?

If your asset receives a **non-certifiable** result, you will receive:
- A list of the specific reasons for refusal
- Guidance on remediation steps for each blocking item
- The option to re-submit after remediation

There is no mandatory waiting period — you can resubmit as soon as the blocking conditions are resolved.
