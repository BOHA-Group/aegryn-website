## What is Aegryn?

Aegryn is a certified tech M&A marketplace operating under Swiss law.
Every asset listed on Aegryn has passed the **CIFS certification**, a rigorous 4-dimension audit covering Code, IP & Rights, Finance, and Security.

This process protects you as a seller by providing institutional-grade documentation that qualified buyers trust.

---

## The 4 Steps to Certification

### Step 1, Submit your asset
Complete the submission form on aegryn.com. Provide a brief description of your asset, its ARR range, and your transaction objectives. Pay the evaluation fee to initiate the process.

### Step 2, Receive your document checklist
Aegryn assigns a certified CIFS expert to your dossier. You will receive the **Data Room Checklist** (see `DATA_ROOM_CHECKLIST.md`) listing all required and recommended documents across the 4 dimensions.

### Step 3, Build your data room
Upload all required documents through your secure seller space. The CIFS expert reviews each document and may request clarifications or additional items.

### Step 4, Receive your grade
Once the audit is complete, Aegryn issues your official grade (B → AEG ★). You receive a full grade report explaining the findings. Your asset is then eligible for the Aegryn catalogue.

---

## What Aegryn Evaluates

Your asset is assessed across 4 dimensions, each equally weighted:

| Dimension | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **C, Code** | Architecture, test coverage, CI/CD, vulnerabilities, documentation, tech debt |
| **I, IP & Rights** | Trademarks, employee IP assignments, licences, litigation status, GDPR compliance |
| **F, Finance** | ARR, churn, NRR, gross margin, growth, client concentration, runway |
| **S, Security** | Pentest, MFA, encryption, GDPR documentation, certifications, incident history |

---

## What Can Block Your Certification

The following situations trigger an **automatic refusal**, your asset cannot be certified until resolved:

- **Code:** Open critical vulnerabilities with no external audit, or test coverage below 10% with many critical vulnerabilities
- **IP:** Active IP litigation with no employee IP assignment contracts
- **Finance:** Runway under 3 months with ARR below €100k, or monthly churn above 15%
- **Security:** Active security incident in progress, or no MFA on admin access with no pentest ever conducted

---

## Tips for a Successful Certification

- Start with `DATA_ROOM_CHECKLIST.md` and assemble all documents before submitting
- Ensure all employee and contractor IP assignment contracts are signed and dated
- Have your ARR independently verified by your accountant before submission
- Run a pentest if you haven't done one recently, it directly impacts your Security score
- Document your tech debt, even if it is significant, undocumented debt scores worse
- GDPR/LPD compliance documentation (register, privacy policy, DPA) must be current

## Confidentiality

Your identity, company name, and financial data are **never disclosed** to buyers without:
1. A signed NDA from you (seller)
2. A signed NDA from the buyer
3. Explicit buyer qualification verified by Aegryn

Buyers receive only anonymised teasers until the double-NDA condition is met.
