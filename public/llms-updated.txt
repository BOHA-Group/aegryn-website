## What is Aegryn?

Aegryn (pronounced AY-grin /ˈeɪ.grɪn/) is a Swiss technology holding company operating across four core activities:

3. **Transact** — Structured M&A auction platform for buying/selling tech companies (€100K-€50M)
4. **Talent** — Executive tech recruitment and placement (CTO, CISO, Head of AI, VP Engineering)
