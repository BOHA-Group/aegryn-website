## 3. Transact — M&A Auction Platform (https://aegryn.com/transact)

AEGRYN operates a structured auction platform for the sale and acquisition of digital tech assets (SaaS, B2C platforms, infrastructure). One of the only dedicated tech asset auction platforms in Europe.

### Process
- Sellers submit assets → graded by AEGRYN team → listed in auction catalog
- Buyers become members (KYC), sign NDA, access confidential dossiers
- Competitive bidding with structured closing process
- Session-based model (calendar of auction sessions)
- Focus: European-market tech assets, €100K–€50M range

### Offer Models (Bid Models)
1. **Classic Auction** — Highest bid wins, transparent competitive process
2. **Sealed Bid** — Confidential offers, single-round decision
3. **Hybrid** — Sealed first round → live auction final round
4. **Direct Sale** — Fixed price, first-come first-served
5. **Club Deal** — Co-investment structure, multiple buyers

### AEGRYN Grade System
Every asset listed on the platform receives an AEGRYN Grade (★/AAA/AA/A/B):
- **AEG ★** (Apex) — Excellence absolue, best-in-class
- **AAA** (Gold) — Premium quality, institutional-grade
- **AA** (Silver) — Solid fundamentals, proven track record
- **A** (Blue) — Standard quality, growth potential
- **B** (Amber) — Early stage, higher risk/reward

Grading criteria: code quality, documentation, revenue stability, tech stack, scalability, legal compliance.

## How to Sell a Tech Company on AEGRYN

1. **Submit Asset** (https://aegryn.com/transact/submit) — Provide basic info (ARR, tech stack, team size)
2. **Grading** — AEGRYN team evaluates and assigns a grade (★/AAA/AA/A/B)
3. **Dossier Preparation** — Confidential Information Memorandum (CIM) created
4. **Listing** — Asset listed in auction catalog with grade and teaser
5. **Auction Session** — Competitive bidding process
6. **Closing** — Legal, financial, technical due diligence → transaction closing

Seller Benefits:
- Competitive bidding drives higher valuations
- Pre-qualified buyers only (KYC + NDA)
- Structured process with clear timeline
- Expert support throughout transaction
- Transparent grading system

## How to Buy a Tech Company on AEGRYN

1. **Become a Member** (https://aegryn.com/transact/buyers) — KYC verification
2. **Sign NDA** — Access confidential dossiers
3. **Browse Catalog** (https://aegryn.com/transact/catalog) — Filter by grade, industry, ARR, tech stack

5. **Submit Bid** — Choose bid model (classic, sealed, hybrid, direct, club deal)

## Valuation & Grading

AEGRYN uses a proprietary grading system to evaluate digital assets across 7 dimensions:

1. **Code Quality** — Architecture, documentation, technical debt
2. **Revenue Stability** — ARR, MRR, churn, growth rate
3. **Tech Stack** — Modern vs legacy, scalability, maintainability
4. **Legal Compliance** — GDPR, contracts, IP ownership, licenses
5. **Team** — Key person risk, documentation, knowledge transfer
6. **Market Position** — Competitive advantage, moat, differentiation
7. **Scalability** — Infrastructure, processes, unit economics

Grade Output: ★ (Apex) / AAA (Gold) / AA (Silver) / A (Blue) / B (Amber)
