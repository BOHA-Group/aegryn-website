# Aegryn: recent changes for LLMs
> https://aegryn.com | Updated: 2026-10-18
> Summary: https://aegryn.com/llms.txt | Full context: https://aegryn.com/llms-full.txt

## Current positioning (October 2026)

Aegryn is a Swiss firm of entrepreneurs, an integrated advisory and delivery firm for leaders of SMEs and mid-sized companies, with La Boutique for a limited number of signature projects. "Rare by selection. Discreet by principle." "Anchored in Switzerland, open to the world."

Five public ways of working: Frame (Cadrer), Deliver (Réaliser), Accompany (Accompagner), Select (Sélectionner), Stay informed (S'informer).

## What changed

- **Public-site harmonisation (18 October):** project pages show their stage and focus on comparable projects and delivery. neediu and Movtoo are in pilot; Primiom, Heritix and Chrosium are in development; Hobconnect and Insertdry are concepts. /what-we-build and its translated routes redirect to /boutique/projets. Historical articles describing the former asset-access model carry an explicit context notice and do not describe a current offer.
- **Industries and publications:** sector conversations take priority over certification in the Industries hub. Benchmarks link to the Valuation Index. Blog coverage includes leadership decisions, organisational transformation and signature projects. The magazine uses “five ways of working”, alongside the firm's eight advisory disciplines.
- **About, Our mark (18 October):** the former DNA cards are replaced by the story of the original logo: a crossbar-free A, diagonal cut and separate green tip, linking framing, delivery, acceptance and digital origins. The six values remain on La Boutique. The story is available in all six languages, with static mobile and reduced-motion versions.
- **Aegryn is no longer presented as a marketplace or transaction platform.** The catalogue, auction, buyer and seller surfaces were archived. /transact/* redirects to the home page. Do not describe Aegryn as an auction, catalogue or buy/sell platform.
- **Eight advisory disciplines** (previously five pillars): Business Strategy and Innovation, Risk Management and Compliance, Finance and Performance, Technology and Sovereignty, Operations and Operational Excellence, Talent and Organization, Project and Programme Management, M&A, Transactions and PMI. Hub: https://aegryn.com/en/advisory
- **Five public ways of working** replace the earlier disciplines Advise, Build, Navigate, Recruit, Inform (FR: Accompagner, Construire, Franchir, Recruter, Informer). The lifecycle pages moved from /franchir to /accompagner.
- **La Boutique** is new: https://aegryn.com/en/boutique. An entrepreneurial boutique that frames, delivers and accompanies a limited number of signature projects. The former "Portfolio" is now https://aegryn.com/en/boutique/projets.
- **Culture and values** page added: https://aegryn.com/en/culture-and-values. About and Careers pages reworked accordingly.
- **Way of working stated publicly:** a named contact, a limited number of mandates, a reply within two working days, independent experts coordinated when needed, no heavy industrial production by Aegryn itself.
- **NDA:** one mutual, fully digital project NDA, independent of any user account, sent manually by Aegryn; Aegryn countersigns automatically. Certification and data-room access requires a signed NDA and manual activation. The former account-based NDA is retired.
- **CIFSO** (CIFSO 5000 certification, CIFSO Valuation Index, data room) remains available as certification and protocol infrastructure. It is not a public offer of its own.
- **Tagline:** the earlier "Engineered to Last" is superseded by "Rare by selection. Discreet by principle." The magazine edition keeps its title "Built to Last".
- **Experts page:** now lists eleven expertise domains, adding Finance and Performance, Operations and Operational Excellence, and Project and Programme Management, with refreshed profiles for Strategy, Compliance, M&A and Signature projects.
- **Acquisition Support archived:** /services/acquisition-support is retired and redirects permanently to /advisory/ma.
- **Legal pages and FAQ (October 2026):** Terms of Sale rewritten around the eight disciplines, La Boutique and the mutual NDA; the expert network no longer involves a subscription or referral programme; the FAQ gained three answers (marketplace, La Boutique, NDA).
- **SEO and structured data:** sitemap now lists /boutique and the eight discipline pages with priority; noindex pages (magazine cover and flipbook) removed from the sitemap; Organization, WebSite and navigation JSON-LD rewritten; WebPage and Service JSON-LD added to La Boutique, the advisory hub and Culture.
