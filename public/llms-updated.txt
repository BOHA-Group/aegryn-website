## Certification CIFSO 5000 (/grade)

Proprietary independent certification — the ownership record ("acte de propriété") of an organisation's value. A watch without its papers loses part of its value; an organisation without certified value sells at a discount.

Protocol CIFSO 5000: 5 audited dimensions, each 0-20 pts, total /100:
- C: Code & Architecture (proprietary codebase or licensed software stack)
- I: IP & Rights
- F: Finance & Metrics
- S: Security, Sovereignty & AI Exposure (incl. AI vendor dependence, AI inventory, DPA)
- O: Organisation, Talent & Succession

5 immutable grades: AEG ★ (Apex, #5ADDA4), AAA (Gold, #C9A84C), AA (Silver, #9BA8B0), A (Blue, #4A90D9), B (Amber, #D4820A)
Regulatory compliance (v4.11): applicable perimeter profiled then validated; controls distributed per dimension: Data Act (C+I), CRA (C), ePrivacy/DSA/P2B/sanctions/AML (I), NIS2/DORA/AI Act (S), compliance governance (O). Non-compliance lowers the score, caps the grade at AA and affects the TRS; the report is never blocked.
At publication: quantified valuation (industry multiple adjusted on the 5 CIFSO scores) + roadmap potential.
MANDATORY for any certified valuation or transmission mandate handled by Aegryn.
Pages: /grade, /grade/brochure, /grade/submit, /grade/partners, /verify

## CIFSO Valuation Index (/valuation)

Benchmark engine branded "Aegryn CIFSO Valuation Index". Automatic collection every Monday 06:00 Europe/Zurich: official open sources (SNB, ECB, Eurostat, listed-company filings — Switzerland and Europe first) + Aegryn curated multiples + published certified dossiers (per-dimension medians/quartiles).

Freemium:
- Free: 15-min self-assessment, deliberately WIDE illustrative range on a subset of benchmarks — no precise estimate without document analysis;
- Subscription (monthly, 3-day free trial, free cancellation before activation — opening soon): full industry multiples (EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA), per-dimension benchmarks, tightened range, certified comparables;
- Certified: quantified valuation with a published grade.
